Services

  • The website andre.technology and the partners site partners.andre.technology describe our services and let you contact us and choose a consultant.
  • Andre AI: the AI maturity diagnostic (maturity.andre.technology) and the AI strategy workspace (strategy.andre.technology), where an AI interview describes your company’s business processes and builds their schemas “as is” and “to be” and an implementation plan.
  • Neuronium: a platform where AI agents and your team run processes; each company has its own workspace at company.neuronium.ai.
  • Consultant services: an expert review of process schemas and a team bootcamp, delivered by certified Andre AI partners.

Partners work under the Partner Program Terms.

Free services

The AI maturity diagnostic and the interview and schema of the first business process in Andre AI are free; no payment details are needed for them. We may change the scope of free services or stop offering them.

Paid services

  • AI Strategy — a one-time purchase: analysis of your company’s business processes in Andre AI, their schemas and an implementation plan, within the limits shown in the product for your order.
  • Neuronium — a subscription, see “Neuronium subscription and automatic renewal”.
  • Schema review — a one-time order: a consultant reviews the schemas you choose and recommends changes. The price depends on the number of schemas at a pace of 10 schemas an hour; a complex schema or an integration beyond the basic review is priced by a separate estimate agreed before payment.
  • Bootcamp — one three-month program: 12 weekly 60-minute sessions for one team. The price is set for a team of 10 people and does not depend on the number of participants. Before it starts, the team and the consultant agree on one or more processes to launch in Neuronium together.
  • Packages: “AI Strategy” — the strategy; “AI Platform” — the strategy and the first month of Neuronium, from the second month the Neuronium subscription at the base price; “AI Transformation” — the strategy, three months of Neuronium and the bootcamp, from the fourth month the Neuronium subscription at the base price. A schema review can be added to any package.
  • For companies with 250 or more employees we prepare an individual estimate.

Neuronium subscription and automatic renewal

  • The subscription is paid monthly or yearly and renews automatically for the same period: the payment is charged to the payment method you used until you cancel the subscription.
  • From the second paid month (from the fourth in the “AI Transformation” package) the subscription is charged at the base price for your company size. If the base price changes, the new price applies from the first renewal after we notify you.
  • You can cancel at any time by writing to admin@andre.technology or in your account where this option is available. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the paid period; until then the workspace works as usual. The current paid period is not refunded, except as described in “Refunds and disputes”.
  • Each month includes a budget for AI model calls made with our keys — on a yearly plan too, the budget is set per month; the workspace shows how much has been used. At 80% we warn you; when the budget is used up, model calls with our keys stop until the next month or a renewal. You can connect your own model API key at any time: calls made with it are not counted against the budget and are paid to that provider under your own agreement with it.
  • If a renewal is not paid, 3 days after the end of the paid period the workspace becomes read-only: you can sign in and read everything but cannot change data or run AI until the subscription is paid. The workspace also becomes read-only when the subscription ends.
