About Me

I build an AI-native ecosystem for human good

I grew up in Ussuriysk, in the Russian Far East — home to the largest tigers on Earth.

My grandfather gave me my first computer when I was three, and artificial intelligence became my chess opponent long before AI became part of everyday life.

I won chess tournaments, as well as mathematics and computer science competitions. At the same time, I spent more than a decade in martial arts and later moved into powerlifting and bodybuilding.

Very early, I learned two things that still define the way I work today:

Intelligence creates possibilities, but discipline turns them into reality.

My path was not a straight line into technology. As a teenager, I wrote for a computer magazine, worked construction jobs, unloaded trucks and even cleaned basements to earn money.

That period shaped another principle I still live by:

Sometimes the strongest motivation is not the dream of a better life, but the determination never to return to the old one.

From Education to AI

Education became my way forward.

  • Bachelor’s degree in Business Informatics — Far Eastern Federal University.
  • Master’s degree in Business Informatics — Higher School of Economics, a leading Russian university, after moving to Moscow.
  • Diploma from the International Institute of Business Analysis.
  • Postgraduate research in AI and data analysis — and then teaching at universities myself.

During this time, I won machine-learning competitions and the National Olympiad in Business Informatics.

University gave me the foundation. The next step was to test it against real business.

From Data to AI Transformation

I started my professional career at Accenture, working with data for major financial institutions.

Later, I joined Sberbank, the largest bank in Eastern Europe, as a Data Engineer. Over the next few years, I progressed to Chief Data Officer and Chief Data Scientist, moving from building data infrastructure to leading large-scale AI transformation.

There, I built analytics and AI systems that helped senior management make better decisions, and led AI adoption across strategic management, macroeconomic analysis, HR, organizational design and thousands of business processes.

$40M+

The work generated more than $40 million in measurable business impact and affected the way hundreds of people worked. I was recognized as one of the bank’s top leaders.

While working at Sberbank, I also won the company’s startup accelerator with my own AI-powered dating service.

That experience made something clear to me: I no longer wanted to build only inside other people’s systems. I wanted to create my own.

And yet, reaching a top management position taught me something even more important:

Status, salary and career success do not necessarily make a person free.

Leaving the Corporate Path

I wanted to create products that could be useful to people around the world. So I left my corporate career, left postgraduate studies and moved to Cyprus.

There, I became CTO of an AI startup studio, building technology across very different domains.

  • Trained self-driving cars in simulation.
  • Built an AI digital twin of the power supply chain that detected electricity losses.
  • Helped create thousands of virtual influencers.
  • Worked on many other AI startups across different industries.
200K+

One of those startups became Product of the Day and Product of the Week on Product Hunt and grew to more than 200,000 users.

Over the years, I learned how to manage teams of engineers, launch products and turn technologies into real businesses.

But then something changed. As AI systems became more capable, I realized that many things that once required entire teams could now be built by one person.

I started learning how to create products and launch businesses without building a traditional team. That completely changed the way I thought about entrepreneurship.

I no longer wanted to build another startup the old way. I wanted to build a system that would allow one person or a small team to create what previously required an entire company.

That idea became Andre AI Technologies.

Building an AI-Native Ecosystem for Human Good

After more than a decade working across corporations, startups, research and education, I decided to bring everything I had learned into one system.

I had seen both sides of the market.

Large companies

Had resources, but often moved too slowly.

Startups

Could move incredibly fast, but with fewer resources, they could not always turn every idea into reality.

My goal became to give solopreneurs and small teams the power of a corporation, while helping established companies gain the speed and adaptability of startups.

I began working with both. I advised startups and large companies on AI strategy, AI product development and AI transformation.

$20M+

Across these projects, the financial impact of the solutions I helped create reached more than $20 million.

But consulting alone could never scale far enough. There is only one of me, and I cannot personally work with every entrepreneur or company that wants to transform.

So I started turning my experience into a system. I developed a methodology for diagnosing businesses, identifying where AI can create real value and building an AI strategy that gives companies a clear direction for transformation.

The strategy becomes the roadmap. The next step is execution.

Now I am building an AI operating system for business, powered by hundreds of specialized AI employees that can help with management, marketing, sales, product development and other areas of business.

But technology alone is not enough. A new economy also requires new skills, new roles and new ways of working.

That is why I began collaborating with universities to help people prepare for emerging AI roles and make applied AI education accessible to more people. I also collaborate with research centers to develop new technologies and turn them into products that create real value.

Education, research, technology, products and business transformation are not separate directions for me. They are parts of the same ecosystem.

I build products for my own work, turn the most useful ones into tools that others can use, and bring together entrepreneurs, researchers and engineers who want to participate in building the next economy.

And I make the educational layer accessible for free. This is what I am building at Andre AI Technologies.

Welcome to the new economy — an economy where people and AI work together for Human Good.
