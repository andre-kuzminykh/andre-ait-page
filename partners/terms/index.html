The program

Partners are independent consultants certified by Andre AI. They review clients’ process schemas, run team bootcamps and help launch processes in Neuronium, and they can bring clients with their own promo code. A partner works on their own account: they are not our employee or representative and cannot make commitments on our behalf.

Becoming a partner

  • Application. You send your name, email, LinkedIn profile, experience and proposed rate.
  • Academy. We invite you to the Andre AI Academy course for AI consultants.
  • Certificate. You take the exam on process analysis and schema review. The Andre AI team checks it manually; if you pass, you become an Andre AI Certified Consultant.
  • Interview. We go through a real case with you and agree on your rate.
  • Partner. You get a partner account, a promo code and a public profile, which appears in the catalog once you turn it on.

We decide at each stage and may decline an application. Your current stage is shown in your partner account.

Partner account

You sign in to the partner account with a code sent to your email. There you manage your profile and rate, see your promo code and client requests, mark a review as delivered and a bootcamp session as held, and see your earnings and payouts by month.

Rate and services

  • You set your own rate, from $50 an hour. A new rate applies to new orders; orders already placed keep the rate fixed in them.
  • A schema review is estimated at 10 schemas an hour. Work beyond a basic review — a complex schema or an integration — is done under a separate estimate agreed with the client before payment.
  • A bootcamp is 12 weekly 60-minute sessions over three months for one client team. Before it starts, you and the team agree on the processes to launch.
  • Other work, such as implementation or support of processes in Neuronium, is done under a separately agreed scope and estimate.

Pay for services

  • The client pays the price of the service: your rate multiplied by the hours of work and divided by 0.70. You receive 70% of that price, which equals your rate multiplied by the hours; Andre AI keeps 30%.
  • A review is earned when you mark it delivered; a bootcamp is earned session by session as you mark sessions held, and for all 12 sessions the sum equals the agreed pay for the course.
  • You receive this pay for every service you deliver, including orders a client places directly from the catalog without a promo code.

Referral commission

  • Clients with your promo code get 10% off the AI Strategy and the first paid month of Neuronium (in the “AI Transformation” package — the first of its three months).
  • You receive 10% of what the client actually paid for these items after the discount, excluding VAT. Example: a $500 strategy costs the client $450 with your code, and you receive $45.
  • Commission is due only on amounts that have been paid and not refunded. If a payment is refunded or disputed, the commission on it is reversed, also after it has been paid out.
  • No commission is due on schema reviews, bootcamps or later months of the subscription, unless we agree otherwise in writing.
  • Referral commission and pay for services are separate: if a client you referred orders a bootcamp from another partner, you receive the commission and that partner receives the pay for the bootcamp. If you run it yourself, you receive both.

Payouts

  • Your earnings are counted in US dollars and shown in your partner account by month and by order.
  • After a month closes, Andre AI Technologies LTD pays the amount due for that month, once a month, to the payment details you provide. Payouts are made manually.
  • You are responsible for correct payment details. We may ask for the information we need to make the payment lawfully, such as an invoice or tax details.
  • If a client’s payment is refunded or disputed after we have paid you, the correction is deducted from your next payouts.

Taxes

Your rate and all amounts you receive are before your taxes. You pay your own taxes, registrations and contributions in your country.

Quality and conduct

  • Deliver services professionally, on time and within the agreed scope; answer client requests within a reasonable time.
  • Keep clients’ data confidential and use it only to deliver the service; do not take it out of Andre AI or Neuronium beyond what the work requires, and follow data protection law.
  • Describe Andre AI, Neuronium, prices and results accurately; do not promise prices, discounts or results that we do not offer, and do not present yourself as our employee.
  • Promote your code honestly: no spam, no misleading ads, no fake reviews.
  • You are responsible for the quality of your services and for your conduct towards clients.

Public profile and reviews

  • Your profile is published in the public catalog — on the partners site and in Andre AI — only after you turn on “show in catalog” in your partner account. You can turn it off at any time.
  • The profile shows your name, headline, about, city and country, languages, services, industries, skills, rate, LinkedIn link, certificates, rating, number of completed projects and client reviews. You confirm that what it says is true and that you may publish it.
  • Certificates are added by us. Clients rate completed services; the rating is the average of published reviews. We may hide a review that is abusive or not about the service, but we do not edit reviews.

Use of our name

While you are a certified partner, you may call yourself an Andre AI Certified Consultant and link to your profile. Any other use of our names and logos requires our permission.

Ending the partnership

  • You may leave the program at any time by writing to admin@andre.technology. We may end or suspend a partnership for a breach of these terms, client complaints we have confirmed, or misconduct.
  • When the partnership ends, your profile is removed from the catalog and your promo code is disabled. Services in progress are completed or, in agreement with the client, passed to another partner or refunded to the client.
  • What you earned for services delivered and the commission on payments received before the end, minus refund corrections, is paid with the next monthly payout.

Changes

We may change these terms, the commission and the program, and we tell partners about changes by email before they take effect. Changes do not affect orders already paid: they keep the terms fixed in them.

Liability

To the extent permitted by law, neither side is liable to the other for indirect losses or lost profits. Our liability to a partner is limited to the amounts due to the partner under these terms.

Personal data and governing law

How we handle the personal data of partners and clients is described in the Privacy Policy; the use of our services is also governed by the Terms of Use. These terms are governed by the law of the country where Andre AI Technologies LTD is registered, unless mandatory law of your country provides otherwise.
