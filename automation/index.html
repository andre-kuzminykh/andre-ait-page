FAQ

Does the team need programming skills?
Deep coding knowledge is not required. The program is built entirely around corporate no/low-code platforms (like n8n, Make, Dify). Cross-functional teams—even without a technical background—can design and assemble fully functioning AI agents.
How is our data security ensured?
We work strictly under an NDA. All practical cases, prototype development, and process analysis are performed within your company's closed, secure environment. We also teach how to deploy LLM/RAG systems on-premise or in private clouds for total isolation.
What exactly will we get in just 1 month?
The intensive format guarantees quick deliverables. You will receive a mapped matrix of prioritized processes, MVPs of 1–2 operational agents, a detailed economic calculation (ROI), and a robust implementation roadmap for the upcoming quarter.
Can we train more than 30 people?
Yes, the program is scalable. We can split the training into multiple streams or conduct mass corporate lectures for a larger audience, allocating a focus group of up to 30 people for the hands-on intensive practice and agent assembly.
