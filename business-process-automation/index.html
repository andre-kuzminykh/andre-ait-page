AI Business Process Automation

Automation fails when it starts from tools and succeeds when it starts from processes. Our approach: understand the work as it is, redesign it for AI, then implement agents that carry the routine — with people supervising what matters.

The method

  • Current-state mapping (AS-IS). Interview the people who do the work and build an honest diagram of today's process: stages, roles, systems, metrics, and pain points.
  • Automation points. Mark the steps where AI removes routine work, ranked by business impact and technical complexity.
  • Future-state design (TO-BE). Redesign the process with AI agents in the flow and explicit human roles at every step.
  • Implementation roadmap. Sequence changes into practical waves — quick wins first, deeper redesign after.
  • Agents in production. Build and connect custom AI agents, then measure speed, consistency, and capacity.

Do it with our tools

This method is built into our products: the AI Strategy tool interviews you about a process, maps AS-IS and TO-BE states, and proposes AI agent opportunities with an implementation roadmap.
