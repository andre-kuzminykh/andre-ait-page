Custom AI Agents for Business Processes

An AI agent is only useful when it is built around a real process — with clear inputs, outputs, and a defined role for the humans who supervise it. Andre AI designs agents starting from your processes, not from a tool catalog.

How we design agents

  • Map the process. Describe how the work actually happens today — stages, roles, systems, and pain points.
  • Find agent opportunities. Identify the steps where an AI agent adds speed, consistency, or capacity.
  • Design the future process. Redesign the workflow with the agent in place and a clear human role — operator, supervisor, manager, or expert.
  • Define agent skills. Specify what the agent must know and do before any code is written.
  • Implement and iterate. Build the agent, connect it to your systems, and improve it against real work.

Agent roles we design for

Operations & management

Task management, coordination, reporting, and internal support workflows.

Marketing & content

Campaign execution, social media content production, and creative assistance.

Sales & support

Lead generation support, customer assistance, and issue resolution.

Analytics & engineering

Data analysis, insight generation, application building, and system integration.
