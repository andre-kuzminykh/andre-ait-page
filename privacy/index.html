Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

This policy explains what personal data Andre AI Technologies LTD (“we”, “us”) collects on andre.technology, why we collect it and what rights you have.

Who we are

Andre AI Technologies LTD operates the website andre.technology and is the controller of the personal data described here. Questions about this policy or your data: admin@andre.technology.

What we collect

  • Data you send us through forms on the site: your name, email, Telegram handle or other contact, your message, the package and company size you picked, and the email you leave to subscribe to news.
  • Technical data that is processed automatically when the page loads: IP address, browser and device type, requested pages and time. It is handled by our hosting and content delivery providers (see below).
  • Settings stored only in your browser (localStorage): the chosen language and, if the network failed, a form you sent that is waiting to be delivered. These never leave your device unless you send the form.

What we do not collect

The site sets no cookies, runs no analytics or advertising trackers and does not build profiles of visitors.

Why we use it

  • To answer your request and arrange an AI diagnostic, strategy session, consultation or other service you asked about (steps taken at your request before a contract).
  • To send our newsletter if you subscribed (your consent; you can unsubscribe at any time).
  • To keep the site secure and working (our legitimate interest).

Who processes it for us

  • FormSubmit (formsubmit.co) delivers form submissions to our mailbox.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the site; Google Fonts and Cloudflare cdnjs deliver fonts and icons.
  • Our email provider stores the messages we receive.

We do not sell personal data and share it only with these providers, or when the law requires it. Some providers are located outside your country; they protect data with standard contractual safeguards.

Our other services

The AI diagnostic (maturity.andre.technology), AI strategy workspace (strategy.andre.technology), Neuronium (neuronium.ai) and our Telegram bot are separate services with their own terms. Links to social networks lead to sites governed by their own policies.

How long we keep it

Requests and correspondence — as long as needed to handle them and for up to 3 years afterwards for our records. Newsletter email — until you unsubscribe. Server logs of our providers — according to their retention rules.

Your rights

You may request access to your data, its correction or deletion, restriction of or objection to processing, and a copy in a portable format; you may withdraw consent at any time. Write to admin@andre.technology — we answer within 30 days. You also have the right to complain to your data protection authority.

Children

The site is intended for businesses and is not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

We may update this policy; the date of the latest version is shown at the top of the page.
