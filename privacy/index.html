Who we are

Andre AI Technologies LTD is the controller of the personal data described in this policy. Questions about this policy or your data: admin@andre.technology.

The policy covers:

  • the website andre.technology and the partners site partners.andre.technology (also available at andre.technology/partners/);
  • Andre AI: the AI maturity diagnostic at maturity.andre.technology and the AI strategy workspace at strategy.andre.technology;
  • Neuronium: each company’s own AI workspace at company.neuronium.ai;
  • the Andre AI partner program and the partner account;
  • our Telegram bot.

Data your company adds

In Andre AI and Neuronium a company describes its work: processes, roles, systems and documents, and in Neuronium also files, connected services and workflows. This content may include personal data of the company’s employees, customers and partners. We process it to provide the service to that company, according to its settings and instructions. The company decides what to add and is responsible for having a legal basis for it and for informing the people concerned. Where the law treats the company as the controller of this content, we process it on the company’s behalf.

The website and the partners site

  • Forms: the name, email, Telegram handle or other contact and the message you send, the package and company size you picked, and the email you leave to subscribe to news. Website forms are delivered to our mailbox by FormSubmit. Forms on the partners site go to Andre AI (strategy.andre.technology) and, if it is unavailable, to our mailbox by FormSubmit.
  • A package button opens Andre AI with the package, company size, billing period and language you chose written in the page address and, if you came by a partner’s link, the partner’s promo code and profile name.
  • Technical data processed automatically when a page loads: IP address, browser and device type, requested pages and time. It is handled by GitHub Pages, which hosts the sites, and by Google Fonts and Cloudflare cdnjs, which deliver fonts and icons. Requests to partners.andre.technology pass through our server, which forwards them to GitHub Pages.
  • Your browser’s local storage (localStorage) keeps the chosen language and, if the network failed, a form waiting to be sent. This never leaves your device unless you send the form.

The two sites set no cookies, run no analytics or advertising trackers and do not build profiles of visitors.

Andre AI

Account

  • Your email and, if you sign in with Google, the name and profile picture Google shares with us. You can also set your name and picture yourself.
  • Sign-in codes we send to your email. A code is valid for 10 minutes and allows a limited number of attempts.
  • Your company, your role in it (founder or invited member) and invitations.

Diagnostic and interview

  • Answers to the AI maturity diagnostic. The diagnostic result is calculated by fixed rules, not by an AI model.
  • Your answers in the interview, typed or spoken. A voice recording is sent to speech recognition and turned into text, which you can correct before sending. We keep the text with your answers and delete the recording once it has been recognised. If recognition fails, the recording is kept on our server for 14 days so that it is not lost, and your browser keeps its own copy until you retry or delete it.
  • What you tell us about the company and what is created from it: company name, size and industry, departments, roles and people as you describe them, processes, systems and documents, process schemas “as is” and “to be”, AI agents, the strategy and the implementation plan.

AI processing

To conduct the interview and build schemas, the strategy and the plan, we send the relevant parts of your answers and company data to AI model providers (currently OpenAI and Anthropic) through their APIs, and voice recordings to OpenAI for speech recognition. The providers process this data to return the result to us. We do not sell your data and do not use it to train AI models.

Purchases

  • Orders: the items, prices, discount, promo code and the partner it belongs to, the consultant and their rate, and the status of payment and refunds.
  • Card payments are made on Stripe’s payment page. Stripe processes your card details; we receive the payment status, the amount and Stripe’s reference numbers. We do not see or store full card numbers.

Usage records

We record how Andre AI is used so that we can find errors and improve the product. These records are kept in our own database; no third-party analytics is used. We record:

  • every click: what was clicked and its visible text (up to 60 characters), and how far you scrolled a screen;
  • how you fill in text fields: the pauses between keystrokes, deletions, the amount of pasted text, the time it took and the final text of the field. For fields with sign-in codes, passwords or payment details we record only length and timing, never the text;
  • the screens you open and the time spent on them, browser errors and network failures, device and browser type;
  • random identifiers of your browser and of the browser tab, kept in its local and session storage, which link these events together, including before you sign in;
  • each request to our server: time, address, response, IP address, browser and the page you came from;
  • each call to an AI model in full: the instructions, the input and the answer, the model, the tokens used and the time it took.

Cookies and browser storage

Andre AI sets one cookie, amx_lang, which remembers the interface language for a year. Your sign-in, company, language, theme, drafts and unsent recordings are kept in the browser’s local storage so that you stay signed in and do not lose your work. We do not use advertising cookies.

Emails

We send sign-in codes and messages about your account, orders and payments through our email provider.

Andre AI and Neuronium

If your company uses Neuronium, Andre AI passes to the company’s Neuronium workspace the processes and AI agents you choose to launch there and the status of the subscription, and can open Neuronium for you with a signed link instead of a separate sign-in.

Neuronium

Each company has its own workspace at company.neuronium.ai. Only the email addresses allowed for the workspace can sign in: with a code sent to the email, with Google or with a link from Andre AI.

  • Workspace content: files and documents, the company’s knowledge, workflows and AI agents, chats with the assistant and the history of runs — steps, results and errors.
  • Connected services. A company can connect Google Workspace through Google sign-in (OAuth) and choose which access to grant, for example to Gmail, Google Drive, Calendar, Sheets, Docs, Slides, Tasks or Forms. Neuronium uses this access only for the tasks and workflows the company sets up, and only while the service stays connected. Access can be revoked at any time in your Google Account (myaccount.google.com/permissions). Keys of other connected services are kept in the workspace’s protected secrets storage and are never shown back.
  • Our use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements: we use it only for the features set up in the workspace, do not use it for advertising, do not sell it, do not use it to train AI models, and people do not read it without your permission unless security or the law requires it.
  • Model usage. Neuronium counts every AI model call — tokens, cost and runs — against the model budget of the company’s plan. Calls go to AI model providers (currently Anthropic and OpenAI). A company may connect its own model API key: the key is kept in the workspace’s secrets storage, is never shown or written to logs, and calls made with it are governed by the company’s own agreement with that provider.

The partner program

Application

  • Your name, email, LinkedIn profile, the experience you describe, the rate you propose and your language.
  • The stages you pass — application, Academy, certificate, interview and our decision — and our correspondence with you.

Partner account

  • You sign in with a code sent to your email. We keep your profile (name, headline, about, city and country, languages, services, industries, skills, rate, LinkedIn), the certificates we issue, your promo code, the orders you brought or delivered, client requests, your earnings and payouts, and the payment details you give us for payouts.

Public profile

Your profile appears in the public catalog on the partners site and in Andre AI only after you turn on “show in catalog” in your partner account, and you can turn it off at any time. The catalog shows your name, headline, about, city and country, languages, services, industries, skills, rate, LinkedIn link, certificates, rating, number of completed projects and reviews.

Client requests and reviews

  • When a client writes to a consultant, we keep the client’s name, email, company and message and pass them to that consultant.
  • When a client orders a schema review or a bootcamp, the consultant gets what is needed to deliver it: the client’s contact details, company and the schemas chosen for review.
  • After a service is completed, the client can rate it from 1 to 5 and write a review. A published review shows the author’s first name, the initial of their surname and the size of the company, not its name. We may hide a review that is abusive or not about the service.

Telegram bot

  • Your Telegram account ID, name, username and language; the messages, voice messages, video messages and files you send to Andre through the bot, and the replies.
  • Orders for payment codes. Payments in roubles are made on Tochka Bank’s payment page: the bank processes the card details and we receive the payment status. The code is then redeemed in Andre AI.

Messages in Telegram are also processed by Telegram under its own policy.

Why we use data

  • To provide the services you use or order: your account, the diagnostic, the interview and the strategy, the Neuronium workspace, orders, consultant services and the partner program (performance of a contract or steps before it).
  • To take payments, keep accounts and pay partners (contract and our legal obligations).
  • To answer your requests (steps taken at your request).
  • To keep the services working and secure, prevent abuse, find and fix errors and improve the product — this is what usage records and AI call logs are for (our legitimate interest).
  • To publish a partner profile and to send our newsletter (your consent, which you can withdraw at any time).

We do not make decisions about you that have legal or similarly significant effects solely by automated means.

Who receives data

We do not sell personal data. We share it only with the following recipients and only as much as each of them needs:

  • Hosting: our servers that run Andre AI, Neuronium and the partners subdomain; GitHub Pages for the websites; Google Fonts and Cloudflare cdnjs for fonts and icons.
  • AI providers: OpenAI (language models, speech recognition and text embeddings for search) and Anthropic (language models).
  • Payments: Stripe; Tochka Bank for payments in roubles in the Telegram bot.
  • Communication: our email provider; FormSubmit for website forms; Telegram for the bot.
  • Google: for sign-in with Google and for the Google Workspace services a company connects to Neuronium.
  • Consultants (partners): client requests and the order details they need to deliver a service.
  • Your company: people invited to your company’s Andre AI account or Neuronium workspace see the company’s shared content.
  • Authorities, when the law requires it.

International transfers

Some of these providers process data outside your country, including in the United States. Where the law requires it, such transfers are protected by contractual safeguards such as standard contractual clauses.

How long we keep data

  • Website requests and correspondence: as long as needed to handle them and up to 3 years afterwards for our records. The same applies to partner applications that did not lead to a partnership.
  • Newsletter email: until you unsubscribe.
  • Andre AI account and company content: while the account exists. At your request we delete them together with the log of requests to our server, except records we must keep by law.
  • Neuronium workspace: while it exists. After a subscription ends, the workspace stays available read-only so that the company can take its data; we delete it at the company’s request.
  • Usage records and AI call logs: 180 days, then they are deleted automatically.
  • Voice recordings: deleted once recognised; a recording that could not be recognised — after 14 days.
  • Orders, payments, partner earnings and payouts: as long as accounting and tax law requires.
  • Partner profile and reviews: while you are a partner; after that we remove the profile from the catalog and keep only what we need for payouts and accounting.
  • Server logs of our providers: according to their retention rules.

Security

Data travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Sign-in codes expire after 10 minutes and allow a limited number of attempts. Keys of connected services and model keys are kept in protected storage and are never shown. Our team’s access to customer data is limited to what is needed for support and fixing errors. No system is completely secure; if a breach affects your data, we will inform you and the authorities as the law requires.

Your rights

You may request access to your data, its correction or deletion, restriction of or objection to processing, and a copy in a portable format; you may withdraw consent at any time. Write to admin@andre.technology — we answer within 30 days. You also have the right to complain to your data protection authority.

Children

Our services are intended for businesses and are not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

We may update this policy; the date of the latest version is shown at the top of the page. If a change materially affects how we use data you have already given us, we will tell you in the product or by email.
